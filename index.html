ABOUT RSHS IX

Preparing students for a world of discovery.

Regional Science High School for Region IX is dedicated to developing students who are passionate about science, mathematics, research, innovation, and learning.

Excellence through science and education.

RSHS provides a learning environment where students can develop their knowledge, skills, creativity, and critical-thinking abilities.

Through academic programs, student organizations, research activities, and community involvement, students are encouraged to become responsible and innovative members of society.
